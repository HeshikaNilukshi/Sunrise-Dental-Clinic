Staff User Guide & Documentation

Step-by-step instructions for clinic receptionists and administrators on managing appointments, patients, and billing workflows in Sunrise Dental Clinic.

1

Register Patient

Capture patient name, unique contact number, email, and home address.

2

Book Appointment

Select patient, dentist, treatment type, and time. Unique APT# is generated.

3

Complete Visit

Mark appointment completed once consultation or dental procedure is done.

4

Bill & Receipt

Auto-calculate fees (Treatment + LKR 500 Consultation), print invoice, and mark paid.

A

1. Patient Registration & Search

Adding New Patients: Navigate to Patients → New Patient. Fill in the full name, contact number, email, and home address. Each contact number must be unique in the system.

Patient Search: You can search for registered patients by name or contact number on the Patients directory page.

Patient History: Click on any patient to view their complete history of past and upcoming appointments.

B

2. Appointment Scheduling & Double-Booking Prevention

Booking a Slot: Go to Appointments → New Appointment. Select the patient, dentist, treatment type, date, and time slot.

Unique Numbering: The system automatically generates a unique tracking number format: APT-YYYYMMDD-NNN (e.g., APT-20260902-001).

Double-Booking Prevention: The system validates dentist availability in real-time and will prevent booking if the selected dentist already has an active appointment at that same date and time.

Cancellation: Appointments can be cancelled with one click, which frees the time slot and dispatches an alert email to the patient.

C

3. Quick Search by Appointment Number

Staff can instantly look up any booking using the Search Tool or the quick search bar in the top navigation/dashboard.

Entering a valid appointment number (e.g., APT-20260902-001) navigates directly to the appointment details page.

D

4. Billing, Fee Calculation & Print Invoices

Fee Structure: Every invoice automatically calculates:
Total Bill = Base Treatment Fee + Consultation Fee (LKR 500.00)

Invoice Generation: Once an appointment is marked COMPLETED, click "Generate Invoice". The system creates an invoice number formatted as INV-YYYYMMDD-NNN.

Printing Receipts: The invoice detail view includes a dedicated "Print Invoice" button that formats a clean A4 receipt without the sidebar or UI controls.

Payment Settlement: Click "Mark as Paid" when the patient settles the bill. A payment confirmation receipt email will be automatically sent to the patient.

E

5. Automated Email Notifications

The system is integrated with the Resend API to deliver asynchronous transactional emails:

  • Booking Confirmation sent immediately upon scheduling
  • Cancellation Notice sent when an appointment is revoked
  • Payment Receipt email sent upon invoice payment settlement
